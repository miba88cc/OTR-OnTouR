About

Milan P. J. Bachofner

Milan Paul Jacques Bachofner was born on October 3, 1988, and grew up in the Netherlands. The son of a Swiss mother and a Czech father, he was shaped by different cultures from an early age. As a young boy, he was blessed in Paris by the Buddhist monk Chao Saysanak Na Champassak. Creativity, drawing, technology, and later a strong desire to explore the world have accompanied him from an early age.

Today, Milan works as an independent visual creator based in Switzerland under OTR-OnTouR by Milan Bachofner. He delivers end-to-end photo and video productions for brands, artists, restaurants, events, and social media — from concept development and production to final post-production.
